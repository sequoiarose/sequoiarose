Sequoia Rose Andrade is a third year PhD Candidate in Statistics at UC Berkeley advised by Professor Philip Stark. Her research focuses on the intersection of computational methods and social good applications, with specific projects on racial justice in the legal system and carbon emissions monitoring.

Before joining Berkeley, she obtained an M.S. in Statistical Data Science with distinction from San Francisco State University and a B.S. in Mathematics and Psychology from Santa Clara University.

Previously, she worked as a research engineer on a contract at NASA Ames Research Center from 2020 to 2025. She is an awardee of the National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship Program (NSF GFRP) and the Berkeley Chancellor's Fellowship.
